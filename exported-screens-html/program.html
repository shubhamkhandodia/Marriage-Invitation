Things the card leaves unsaid

Six small notes,
so you land softly

01

“Onwards” is the schedule

Rites begin at the printed hour and continue until they are finished. Come at the start time to see them; come an hour later if you would rather eat first.

02

Dress for the day, not the week

Tuesday is turmeric and henna — old cotton, sleeves you do not mind losing. Wednesday is festive: saree, lehenga, kurta-pyjama or a bandhgala in maroon, gold or deep green.

03

Two cities, one plan

Dehradun (U.K.) on the 30th and 1st, Gurugram (Haryana) on the 2nd. Settle the travel between them before you call the host.

04

Shagun, not gifts

No registry, no boxed list. A sealed envelope handed to the elders, or a blessing said aloud to the couple, is exactly what is wanted.

05

Photographs

Click freely through the haldi, mehandi and milni. During the saat phere, put the flash away and let the official photographer hold the front row.

06

Children and elders

Both are welcome and both are fed. Seating at the kirtan and mehandi is floor-level, so offer an arm to whoever needs help getting up.
