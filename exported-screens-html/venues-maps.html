How to read these maps

  • Rapid Metro · Sikanderpur≈ 4 km · 12 minAutos outside the exit; the ride in is cheaper and faster than a cab after 6 p.m.
  • Yellow Line · Sultanpur (Huda City Centre)≈ 4 km · 14 minPark at the metro, come the last stretch by auto. Best option if you are staying in Gurgaon.

Good to know

  • Give the driver “Krishna Chowk, then Kartarpuri Road” — Block H addresses come after the chowk.
  • Kartarpuri Road narrows past the shops; keep your car’s right side to the wall.
  • Ground parking is limited — the elders’ drop-off is at the main gate, everything beyond is walk-in.
  • Ring the host on arrival if the gate is decorated; the security desk holds no guest list.

01

By road

6–7 hours

NH-34 to NH-44 down through Yamunanagar, Karnal and Panipat. Leave Regal Heritage by 9 p.m. on the 1st and you are in Gurugram around 4 a.m. Two drivers make this comfortable; one makes it foolish.

02

By train

≈ 4 h + 1.5 h cab

Dehradun to Delhi in the daytime on the Shatabdi or Vande Bharat, then a cab across the city to Gurugram. Book the berth weeks ahead — December weekends clear the Doon trains fast.

03

By air

≈ 50 min + transfers

Jolly Grant to IGI, then 45–60 minutes to Kartarpuri Road. Only worth it if the flight is on time and you have someone waiting at the terminal with a car.
