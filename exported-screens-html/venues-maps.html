How to reach

  • Jolly Grant Airport (DED)≈ 9 km · 25 minPrepaid taxi at the arrival lobby; ask for Telpur Chowk, not the venue name.
  • Dehradun Railway Station (DDN)≈ 5 km · 15 minAutos and Ola/Uber are waiting outside; the lane in from the chowk is one-way at peak.
  • ISBT Karanpur (bus stand)≈ 7 km · 20 minBest if you have come in on an overnight Volvo from Delhi.
  • Host’s home · Balaji Enclave≈ 3 km · 10 minShimla By-pass Road — the host and his family stay here through the week.

Good to know

  • Say “Telpur Chowk” to any Doonwala — the chowk is the landmark, the venue is just off it.
  • Courtyard parking fills by 10:30 a.m. on the 1st; drop elders at the gate, then park on the by-pass and walk in.
  • Monday’s kirtan is held here too — the same venue for all three Dehradun days.

How to reach

  • IGI Airport, Terminal 3 (DEL)≈ 22 km · 50 minLeave by 5.00 p.m. for a 7.00 p.m. start — the Airport–Gurugram road slows badly at dusk.
  • Rapid Metro · Sikanderpur≈ 4 km · 12 minAutos outside the exit; the ride in is cheaper and faster than a cab after 6 p.m.
  • Yellow Line · Sultanpur (Huda City Centre)≈ 4 km · 14 minPark at the metro, come the last stretch by auto. Best option if you are staying in Gurgaon.
  • Gurugram Junction (GGJ)≈ 3 km · 10 minClosest rail halt for local trains from Delhi.
  • Old Gurugram Bus Stand≈ 2 km · 8 minState buses from Delhi and the UP towns drop you here.

Good to know

  • Give the driver “Krishna Chowk, then Kartarpuri Road” — Block H addresses come after the chowk.
  • Kartarpuri Road narrows past the shops; keep your car’s right side to the wall.
  • Ground parking is limited — the elders’ drop-off is at the main gate, everything beyond is walk-in.
  • Ring the host on arrival if the gate is decorated; the security desk holds no guest list.
